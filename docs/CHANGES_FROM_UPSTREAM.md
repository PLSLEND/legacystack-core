# Changes from upstream Liquity V1 (audit map)

Every modification to `src/upstream`, with the LegacyStack decision behind it. Diff against the first commit
("Upstream import") to see the exact lines.

| # | Change | Files | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fixed-rate debt index, 1.5%/yr.** Trove debt, pool debt and `L_LUSDDebt` are stored in normalized units; actual debt = normalized × `rateIndex` / 1e18. `currentRateIndex()` projects the index to now; `accrueInterest()` writes it and mints the accrued interest (rounded up) to `interestRecipientAddress` (Phase 0: the fee sink; Phase 1: the Redemption Buffer). Called at the start of every state-changing operation (open/adjust/close via BorrowerOperations; liquidate, batch liquidate, redeem in TroveManager). Rounding policy: debt added to a trove rounds down, debt removed rounds up, so total supply always covers total debt (invariant tested, fuzzed). | TroveManager, ActivePool, DefaultPool, LUSDToken (TroveManager may mint), BorrowerOperations (accrue calls), MultiTroveGetter, ITroveManager, IPool | LS_06 #104, #105, #106; LS_22 row 2 |
| 2 | **ERC-20 collateral (wstETH) instead of native ETH.** Pools hold the token; `sendETH` transfers tokens and notifies pool recipients via `receiveCollateral`; `receive()` fallbacks removed; BorrowerOperations pulls collateral with `transferFrom` (`openTrove`, `addColl`, `adjustTrove` take an amount; `moveETHGainToTrove` takes the gain amount after the Stability Pool approves it). | 7 | **Price feed replaced.** Upstream's Chainlink/Tellor switching feed is deleted. `src/legacystack/PriceFeed.sol` prices wstETH as Chainlink ETH/USD × Lido `stEthPerToken()`, with breakers (stale round, broken answer, 50% round-to-round jump, Lido call failure, Lido rate outside a 1% band that widens 0.1% per day). Any breaker puts the feed into a **degraded** state: `fetchPrice()` returns the last good price and `isReduceOnly()` is true. It heals by itself; there is no key. Note: at a Chainlink proxy phase change the previous round lookup fails for one round, so the feed is degraded for about one heartbeat. | PriceFeed (new), IPriceFeed, PriceFeedTestnet (mock gains a switch) | SR-1; LS_06 #98, #141; LS_22 row 10 |
| 8 | **Reduce-only state.** While the feed is degraded: `openTrove`, debt increases, collateral withdrawals, redemptions and the buffer's standing offer revert; repay, add collateral, close, Stability Pool deposits and withdrawals, and liquidations (at the last good price) stay open. Succession settlement also waits for a healthy feed. | BorrowerOperations, TroveManager, RedemptionBuffer | LS_06 #141 (decided 31 Oct 2026) |
| 9 | **Redemption Buffer** (`src/legacystack/RedemptionBuffer.sol`) replaces LQTY staking as the recipient of everything: OCT mint fees, OCT interest, wstETH redemption fees. It holds a wstETH pot and an OCT inventory. Redemptions are served by the buffer first (OCT moved into inventory with `sendToPool`, collateral sent net of the redemption fee); only the remainder reaches user vaults. The baseRate update sees the whole redemption. Standing offer: anyone buys inventory at oracle par + 0.5% for wstETH. Anyone can fill the pot; nobody can withdraw. Surplus above 200% of target (15% of circulating OCT) is released at most 5% of the pot per day to a fixed sink. `HintHelpers.getRedemptionHints` accounts for the buffer. | RedemptionBuffer (new), TroveManager (`redeemCollateral`, `ContractsCache`, `RedemptionTotals`), BorrowerOperations (`_triggerBorrowingFee`), LUSDToken (`sendToPool` callable by TroveManager), HintHelpers | LS_06 #100-#102; LS_22 rows 5, 7 |
| 10 | **Debt ceiling** = $1,000,000 + $100 per $1 of wstETH in the buffer's pot, checked on every open and debt increase. | BorrowerOperations, RedemptionBuffer (`debtCeiling`) | LS_06 #105; LS_22 row 6 |
| 11 | **Succession Registry** (`src/legacystack/SuccessionRegistry.sol`): one liveness record per owner; up to 10 heirs by basis points plus a fallback heir; one year proof period, 30-day return window; `setPlan` starts the clock and `proveLife` is the only call that resets it (button-only liveness, #160): no vault, pool or third-party action counts, so automation can never look like a living owner. `execute` is permissionless and retryable: the estate's Stability Pool deposit is withdrawn first, the vault's debt is settled (the estate's own OCT first, free; then the buffer's inventory at its standing-offer price, par + 0.5%, what any borrower pays; then a Stability Pool offset at par + 2%, the depositors' pay for providing the OCT, `SETTLE_POOL_PREMIUM`), the vault is closed (`Status.closedBySuccession`), collateral and OCT are pushed to the heirs, failed pushes go to the fallback heir and then to escrow, the executor is paid a bounded reward. Partial settlement leaves the vault open with a higher collateral ratio, and never below the minimum net debt (a short day settles only down to 1,800 OCT, or nothing). An estate under 110% is never settled at par: it is a liquidation like any other vault (an outside reader's point, KupiaSec, 10 Nov 2026). In wind-down the settlement price is the last good price with a 20% haircut, capped so the collateral still covers the debt plus the pool's premium. | SuccessionRegistry (new), TroveManager (`settleForSuccession`, status enum), StabilityPool (`withdrawForSuccession`), BorrowerOperations (`claimCollateralForSuccession`), CollSurplusPool (`claimCollTo`) | LS_06 #135, #136, #160, #170; LS_22 rows 12-13 |
| 12 | **Collateral gifts.** `BorrowerOperations.addCollateralFor(owner, upperHint, lowerHint, amount)`: anyone may add collateral to any active vault from their own balance (`transferFrom(msg.sender)`), also in the reduce-only oracle state. The authorisation rule in `_adjustTrove` is one line: a caller who is not the owner may only add collateral. No operator registry, no delegated borrowing, no auth surface; a third party can make a vault safer, never riskier, and nothing a third party does counts as the owner's proof of life. (Aave and Compound have `onBehalfOf` supplies; the family-rescue framing inside an inheritance protocol is ours.) | BorrowerOperations | LS_06 #160, #161 |
| 14 | **Front-end kickback machinery removed from the Stability Pool.** Liquity's front-end registration, kickback rates, deposit tags, front-end stakes and snapshots, and the split of issuance gains between depositor and front end are gone (they only existed to pay LQTY to referring front ends). `provideToSP(uint)` takes no tag; `getDepositorLQTYGain` is the full share; the issuance plumbing (G sum, `CommunityIssuance` interface) stays for the STACK emissions peripheral. Contract shrinks by 2.7 KB; nothing else in the pool changes. | StabilityPool, IStabilityPool | #153 (j), Luka's yes 5 Nov 2026 |
| 13 | **LQTY removed from the core.** `lqtyToken`, `lqtyStaking` and the LQTY interfaces are gone; the deployment timestamp for the bootstrap check lives in TroveManager. `CommunityIssuance` remains an interface filled by a stub until the STACK emissions peripheral exists. | TroveManager, ITroveManager, interfaces | LS_22 row 16 |
| 15 | **Keeper desk** (`src/periphery/KeeperDesk.sol`, periphery, not core): one call runs the categories the caller chooses (accrue interest; liquidate the vaults passed in; execute the successions passed in; skim the vaults passed in; and, with `tend`, the reserve's housekeeping: release a due surplus, split it, let the POL keeper work and harvest its fees) and sweeps the rewards the protocol already pays (Liquity's 200 OCT + 0.5% gas compensation, the registry's executor reward, the skim cut, the splitter's caller cut) to the caller, so a frequent job such as a liquidation pays only for itself. Holds nothing between calls, has no owner, changes no rule; every step is try/catch so a run always finishes. After each succession it executes it calls the liquidity desk's `inherit(owner)` when that owner has a position (#172), so one press settles the estate and hands its protocol liquidity to the heirs; the desk address is an immutable (zero on a chain without a pool). `pending(maxScan)` scans the sorted list from the riskiest end and reports what a run would do, roughly what it pays, and whether the reserve side has anything to tend. | KeeperDesk (new); no core change | LS_06 #157 (5), Luka's yes 9 Nov 2026; categories #169; desk inherit #172 |
| 16 | **Testnet-only contracts** (`src/testnet/`): `MockWstETH` gains Lido's `receive()` behaviour (ETH in, wstETH out at the current rate; parked test ETH recyclable by the deployer) so the frontend's "convert ETH" step is a plain transfer, as on mainnet; `MockUSDe` (18 decimals, faucet) exists so a Uniswap v3 OCT/USDe pool can be seeded at par by the deploy script. Neither is deployed on mainnet. | MockWstETH, MockUSDe, Deploy.s.sol | LS_06 #157 (2), (10) |
| 17 | **Auto-repay from staking rewards** (`BorrowerOperations.setAutoRepay`, `skimYield`; `RedemptionBuffer.serveSkim`). Each vault carries a baseline: its collateral in stETH units (wstETH x Lido rate) at the last sync; deposits and withdrawals move the baseline with them, redistribution gains are added to it, and a baseline above the vault (after a redemption) is clamped. The skimmable yield is the wstETH above the baseline at today's rate, i.e. exactly what Lido's rate growth added. `skimYield(owner)` sells it to the reserve's standing offer (par + 0.5%, inventory permitting), burns the OCT against the vault's debt straight from the reserve's balance (never below the minimum net debt; any excess goes to the owner's wallet), and leaves the vault at its baseline. The owner may call it at any time with no cut; anyone else only when the owner opted in with a cadence (monthly 30 d, quarterly 91 d, yearly 365 d), a full period has passed, and the OCT covers the period's interest at the fixed rate; the caller's cut is 1% of the OCT, capped at 5 OCT. Blocked in Recovery Mode and in the reduce-only oracle state; the ratio must stay above MCR (it improves whenever the vault is above 100.5%). | BorrowerOperations, RedemptionBuffer (`serveSkim`, BorrowerOperations-only), KeeperDesk (`skimmable`, due skims in `pending`/`run`) | LS_06 #160, #161 |
| 18 | **Reserve waterfall and reserve deposits** (`RedemptionBuffer`). Waterfall: `releasable(price)` is computed on the protocol's OWN wstETH (the pot minus depositors' principal): half of the band between 100% and 200% of target plus everything above 200%, released by anyone at most once a day and at most 5% of the own pot per day, to the surplus sink (set once in `setAddresses`, a periphery splitter). Deposits: `deposit(amount)` keeps the wstETH the depositor's property (`depositOf`, `totalDeposits`), it serves redemptions like the rest of the pot and counts for the debt ceiling, is locked 180 days from the last deposit, and `withdraw(amount)` is allowed only while the pot stays at or above target afterwards. Time served is counted in wstETH-seconds (`pointsOf`, `checkpoint`); the core knows no reward token. The old plain "above 200% only" rule and the immutable-constructor sink are gone. | RedemptionBuffer | LS_06 #160, #161 |
| 19 | **Burn auction** (`src/periphery/BurnAuction.sol`, periphery): the STACK sink that needs no venue. Holds the wstETH the splitter sends it and sells it lot by lot (lot size fixed at deployment) in rolling Dutch auctions paid in STACK; the price per wstETH falls linearly over 48 h to a tenth of the lot's start, then holds; the STACK is burned (`burn`), the wstETH goes to the buyer, the next lot starts at twice the last clearing price. `notify()` restarts the clock when wstETH arrives after an empty spell. No owner, no oracle, no pool. | BurnAuction (new) | LS_06 #160 |
| 20 | **Surplus splitter and POL keeper** (`src/periphery/SurplusSplitter.sol`, `src/periphery/PolKeeperV3.sol`, periphery). The splitter pays whoever calls `split()` 0.1% of what it splits (`CALLER_BPS`, at most `CALLER_CAP` 0.02 wstETH) and then sends the rest by the POL target (#172): while the protocol-owned liquidity is below its target, all of it to the POL keeper (`polFirst()`, read from the keeper's `belowTarget()`; false without a pool or when the read fails); once the target is reached, half to the burn auction and half to the keeper. The keeper converts its wstETH into OCT through the reserve's standing offer (an internal price; the wstETH returns to the reserve, the reserve's inventory becomes the liquidity) and deposits the OCT one-sided into the market pool only when the pool's hour-long time-weighted price has OCT at or above par (adding OCT below par would push it further down, so it waits); the position is held forever. Fees: `harvest()` collects what the positions earned; counter-asset fees buy OCT inside the pool only while OCT is at or below par and never more than 1% over it (otherwise they wait); the OCT then compounds into a new position while the protocol-owned liquidity is below its target and goes to the reserve's inventory once the target is reached (#172). The target: `polTarget()` = `POL_TARGET_BPS` (10%) of the reserve's `circulatingSupply()`; the protocol-owned liquidity `polValue()` = the seed plus every OCT the keeper placed, in OCT, an internal count nobody else can move. Because the target follows the circulating OCT, growth reopens compounding and a shrinking supply sends more to the reserve; minting OCT to lift the target costs the mint fee and moves nothing out of the protocol. `protocolShare()` (the protocol's part of what the pool holds) stays a view. A rule, not a key: fees can only sit in the pool or in the protocol. The pool's opening liquidity is placed by the keeper itself: the deployment sends it OCT and the counter-asset and calls `seed()` once (deployer only, once; the deployer can do nothing else), a two-sided position around par held forever and counted as the protocol's. If any pool step fails the conversion is undone and the wstETH waits with the keeper for the next run; after 90 days of failing it goes back to the reserve pot, so a dead venue can never trap funds and nothing is thrown away. The v3 flavour is the TESTNET stand-in; the mainnet venue is a Curve StableSwap-NG pool and gets a Curve flavour with the same shape. Without a pool the splitter sends both halves to the auction. | SurplusSplitter (new), PolKeeperV3 (new), KeeperDesk (`tend` splits, lets the keeper work and harvests) | LS_06 #161, #165, #169, #170, #172 |
| 21 | **Reserve rewards** (`src/periphery/ReserveRewards.sol`, periphery): pays a fixed amount of STACK per wstETH-second of reserve service (`pointsOf` from the core) out of whatever it holds, first come first served; unpaid points stay owed. Anyone funds it; no owner. The testnet `MockStack` has a faucet and `burn()`; the mainnet STACK is fixed-supply. | ReserveRewards (new), MockStack (testnet) | LS_06 #160, #161 |
| 22 | **Oracle: two ETH/USD networks, the wind-down end state, the direction-aware market price** (`PriceFeed`, `BorrowerOperations`, `RedemptionBuffer`). ETH/USD is read from Chainlink and Chronicle (`readWithAge`, 18 decimals, staleness by age): the mean when both are live and within 3% of each other, the survivor when one is dead, degraded when they disagree or both are dead; times Lido's rate as before, with the rate band. WIND-DOWN: when no ETH/USD source answers and the last good price is 30 days old, `status` becomes `windDown`, permanently: `isReduceOnly()` stays true (no new borrowing, no withdrawals, no redemptions, no standing offer), repay/add collateral/close/liquidate stay open at the last good price, and `settleForSuccession` proceeds at 80% of the last good price (more collateral per OCT to the reserve and the Stability Pool, never less). DIRECTION-AWARE PRICE: `lowerOfMarket(canonical)` returns the lower of the canonical price and Chainlink stETH/USD x rate when that market feed is live (ignored when absent, stale or broken); BorrowerOperations uses it for every action that opens risk (openTrove, debt increases, collateral withdrawals, the yield skim's sale) and the reserve's `offerPrice()` uses it for `buyInventory` and `serveSkim`; redemptions, liquidations and settlement read the canonical price only. The feed's constructor takes the Chronicle and the stETH/USD aggregator addresses (zero = absent); the testnet deploys a `MockChronicle` seeded at the aggregator's price. | PriceFeed, IPriceFeed (`isWindDown`, `lowerOfMarket`), BorrowerOperations, RedemptionBuffer (`offerPrice`), MockChronicle (testnet) | LS_06 #160, #161, #162 |
| 23 | **Reserve fee sharing and the deposit's succession** (`RedemptionBuffer`, `TroveManager`, `SuccessionRegistry`). The wstETH the reserve earns for serving the market, the redemption fees (both the reserve-served part, kept in the pot, and the vault-served part, which `TroveManager` now announces with `shareRedemptionFee` after sending it) and the premium above par of every standing-offer sale (`buyInventory`, `serveSkim`), is shared with the reserve depositors: their part is `depositorsShareBps()`, the depositors' share of the pot and at least half (`DEPOSITORS_MIN_SHARE_BPS`), measured before the pot pays a redeemer; nothing when there are no deposits. The part is set aside as `feesOwed` (a per-deposit accumulator, `feePerDeposit`, checkpointed on every deposit and withdrawal), excluded from `pot()` and therefore from redemption capacity, the ceiling, the waterfall and the target test, and claimable in wstETH at any time (`claimFees`). Interest and mint fees stay the protocol's. Succession (#172): `execute` PAYS OUT a dead depositor's deposit to the heirs' wallets (`payoutForSuccession`, registry only): the earned fees first, in wstETH; then the principal in wstETH as far as the pot stays at or above its target without it; then in OCT from the inventory at the oracle price; only what cannot be paid out yet stays a deposit and passes to the heirs by their shares as deposits (`transferDepositForSuccession`: principal, fees and points pro rata, the heir inherits the later lock). The paid-out part leaves the pot like a withdrawal; the points of a paid-out deposit end with the owner (STACK bootstrap only). The registry's `setAddresses` takes the reserve; the reserve's takes the registry. | RedemptionBuffer, IRedemptionBuffer, TroveManager (one call), SuccessionRegistry (step 4 payout, `_passDeposit` for the rest), ISuccessionRegistry | LS_06 #169, #171, #172 |
| 24 | **Liquidity desk** (`src/periphery/LiquidityDesk.sol`, periphery). OCT/USDe liquidity provided through the protocol so that it belongs to the owner's succession plan. One Uniswap v3 position per owner in the band 0.98 to 1.02 around par (`BAND_TICKS` 200), held by the desk: `provide` (mint or increaseLiquidity, refunds what the pool cannot place; only while the pool's tick is inside the band, so nobody provides at a manipulated price), `collect` (fees to the owner), `withdraw` (part or all, with the fees; an emptied position is burned). `inherit(owner)`, permissionless once `successionState(owner)` is 4 (executed): the position is unwound and the OCT and USDe pushed to the heirs by their shares, to the fallback heir when a push fails, to escrow when that fails too (`claimEscrow`). The keeper desk calls it right after `execute` (#172), so the heirs receive the liquidity in the same press that settles the estate. No owner, no parameter. Testnet flavour (v3 NFTs); the mainnet Curve flavour holds LP tokens with the same shape. | LiquidityDesk (new), Deploy.s.sol (`liquidityDesk`, deployed before the keeper desk), KeeperDesk | LS_06 #171, #172 |
