# Changes from upstream Liquity V1 (audit map)

Every modification to `src/upstream`, with the LegacyStack decision behind it. Diff against the first commit
("Upstream import") to see the exact lines.

| # | Change | Files | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fixed-rate debt index, 1.5%/yr.** Trove debt, pool debt and `L_LUSDDebt` are stored in normalized units; actual debt = normalized × `rateIndex` / 1e18. `currentRateIndex()` projects the index to now; `accrueInterest()` writes it and mints the accrued interest (rounded up) to `interestRecipientAddress` (Phase 0: the fee sink; Phase 1: the Redemption Buffer). Called at the start of every state-changing operation (open/adjust/close via BorrowerOperations; liquidate, batch liquidate, redeem in TroveManager). Rounding policy: debt added to a trove rounds down, debt removed rounds up, so total supply always covers total debt (invariant tested, fuzzed). | TroveManager, ActivePool, DefaultPool, LUSDToken (TroveManager may mint), BorrowerOperations (accrue calls), MultiTroveGetter, ITroveManager, IPool | LS_06 #104, #105, #106; LS_22 row 2 |
| 2 | **ERC-20 collateral (wstETH) instead of native ETH.** Pools hold the token; `sendETH` transfers tokens and notifies pool recipients via `receiveCollateral`; `receive()` fallbacks removed; BorrowerOperations pulls collateral with `transferFrom` (`openTrove`, `addColl`, `adjustTrove` take an amount; `moveETHGainToTrove` takes the gain amount after the Stability Pool approves it). Internal names keep Liquity's "ETH" wording for diff-minimality; they denote collateral-token units. | ActivePool, DefaultPool, CollSurplusPool, StabilityPool, BorrowerOperations, interfaces | LS_19 Δ1; LS_22 row 1 |
| 3 | **Redemption fee floor 0.75%** (was 0.5%). | TroveManager | LS_19 D1 (proposed), LS_18 §E, LS_21 |
| 4 | **Minimum redemption 5,000 OCT.** | TroveManager | LS_06 #103 |
| 5 | **Bootstrap period is a constructor parameter** (was a 14-day constant); 0 on testnets; mainnet value is a D-item (recommendation: none). | TroveManager | LS_06 #123, #137 |
| 6 | **Token name and symbol "OCT"** (was "LUSD Stablecoin"/"LUSD"). | LUSDToken | wording rule |
| 7 | **Price feed replaced.** Upstream's Chainlink/Tellor switching feed is deleted. `src/legacystack/PriceFeed.sol` prices wstETH as Chainlink ETH/USD × Lido `stEthPerToken()`, with breakers (stale round, broken answer, 50% round-to-round jump, Lido call failure, Lido rate outside a 1% band that widens 0.1% per day). Any breaker puts the feed into a **degraded** state: `fetchPrice()` returns the last good price and `isReduceOnly()` is true. It heals by itself; there is no key. Note: at a Chainlink proxy phase change the previous round lookup fails for one round, so the feed is degraded for about one heartbeat. | PriceFeed (new), IPriceFeed, PriceFeedTestnet (mock gains a switch) | SR-1; LS_06 #98, #141; LS_22 row 10 |
| 8 | **Reduce-only state.** While the feed is degraded: `openTrove`, debt increases, collateral withdrawals, redemptions and the buffer's standing offer revert; repay, add collateral, close, Stability Pool deposits and withdrawals, and liquidations (at the last good price) stay open. Succession settlement also waits for a healthy feed. | BorrowerOperations, TroveManager, RedemptionBuffer | LS_06 #141 (decided 31 Oct 2026) |
| 9 | **Redemption Buffer** (`src/legacystack/RedemptionBuffer.sol`) replaces LQTY staking as the recipient of everything: OCT mint fees, OCT interest, wstETH redemption fees. It holds a wstETH pot and an OCT inventory. Redemptions are served by the buffer first (OCT moved into inventory with `sendToPool`, collateral sent net of the redemption fee); only the remainder reaches user vaults. The baseRate update sees the whole redemption. Standing offer: anyone buys inventory at oracle par + 0.5% for wstETH. Anyone can fill the pot; nobody can withdraw. Surplus above 200% of target (15% of circulating OCT) is released at most 5% of the pot per day to a fixed sink. `HintHelpers.getRedemptionHints` accounts for the buffer. | RedemptionBuffer (new), TroveManager (`redeemCollateral`, `ContractsCache`, `RedemptionTotals`), BorrowerOperations (`_triggerBorrowingFee`), LUSDToken (`sendToPool` callable by TroveManager), HintHelpers | LS_06 #100-#102; LS_22 rows 5, 7 |
| 10 | **Debt ceiling** = $1,000,000 + $100 per $1 of wstETH in the buffer's pot, checked on every open and debt increase. | BorrowerOperations, RedemptionBuffer (`debtCeiling`) | LS_06 #105; LS_22 row 6 |
| 11 | **Succession Registry** (`src/legacystack/SuccessionRegistry.sol`): one liveness record per owner; up to 10 heirs by basis points plus a fallback heir; one year proof period, 30-day return window; owner-key actions in BorrowerOperations, StabilityPool and the registry reset the clock; operator actions never do. `execute` is permissionless and retryable: the estate's Stability Pool deposit is withdrawn first, the vault's debt is settled at par (estate OCT, then buffer inventory, then a Stability Pool offset at par), the vault is closed (`Status.closedBySuccession`), collateral and OCT are pushed to the heirs, failed pushes go to the fallback heir and then to escrow, the executor is paid a bounded reward. Partial settlement leaves the vault open with a higher collateral ratio. | SuccessionRegistry (new), TroveManager (`settleForSuccession`, status enum), StabilityPool (`withdrawForSuccession`, touch calls), BorrowerOperations (touch calls, `claimCollateralForSuccession`), CollSurplusPool (`claimCollTo`) | LS_06 #135, #136; LS_22 rows 12-13 |
| 12 | **Operator role.** `BorrowerOperations.operatorAdjustTrove(owner, ...)`: an operator named by the owner may add collateral and repay from its own balance; if the owner allowed it, also borrow and withdraw collateral, which always go **to the owner**, while the vault keeps the owner's CR floor. Operators cannot close the vault, change heirs or reset the clock. Pulls come from `msg.sender`, pushes go to the vault owner, in every path. | BorrowerOperations, SuccessionRegistry | LS_06 #129; LS_22 row 14 |
| 14 | **Front-end kickback machinery removed from the Stability Pool.** Liquity's front-end registration, kickback rates, deposit tags, front-end stakes and snapshots, and the split of issuance gains between depositor and front end are gone (they only existed to pay LQTY to referring front ends). `provideToSP(uint)` takes no tag; `getDepositorLQTYGain` is the full share; the issuance plumbing (G sum, `CommunityIssuance` interface) stays for the STACK emissions peripheral. Contract shrinks by 2.7 KB; nothing else in the pool changes. | StabilityPool, IStabilityPool | #153 (j), Luka's yes 5 Nov 2026 |
| 13 | **LQTY removed from the core.** `lqtyToken`, `lqtyStaking` and the LQTY interfaces are gone; the deployment timestamp for the bootstrap check lives in TroveManager. `CommunityIssuance` remains an interface filled by a stub until the STACK emissions peripheral exists. | TroveManager, ITroveManager, interfaces | LS_22 row 16 |
| 15 | **Keeper desk** (`src/periphery/KeeperDesk.sol`, periphery, not core): one call runs every pending housekeeping action (accrue interest, release reserve surplus if due, liquidate the vaults passed in, execute the successions passed in) and sweeps the rewards the protocol already pays (Liquity's 200 OCT + 0.5% gas compensation, the registry's executor reward) to the caller. Holds nothing between calls, has no owner, changes no rule; every step is try/catch so a run always finishes. `pending(maxScan)` scans the sorted list from the riskiest end and reports what a run would do and roughly what it pays. | KeeperDesk (new); no core change | LS_06 #157 (5), Luka's yes 9 Nov 2026 |
| 16 | **Testnet-only contracts** (`src/testnet/`): `MockWstETH` gains Lido's `receive()` behaviour (ETH in, wstETH out at the current rate; parked test ETH recyclable by the deployer) so the frontend's "convert ETH" step is a plain transfer, as on mainnet; `MockUSDe` (18 decimals, faucet) exists so a Uniswap v3 OCT/USDe pool can be seeded at par by the deploy script. Neither is deployed on mainnet. | MockWstETH, MockUSDe, Deploy.s.sol | LS_06 #157 (2), (10) |

Not yet changed: protocol backstop vault before redistribution (D5 open); STACK emissions contract replacing
the issuance stub; 30%/epoch redemption fatigue cap on user vaults (#98); tBTC branch.
