Grow. Borrow. Pass it on.

A borrowing protocol for people who think in decades. You keep the asset that grows, you borrow against it instead of selling it, and the vault knows what to do when you are no longer there.

Fixed rate. No account. No committee. Rules written once, in code, and never changed.

The strategy, in three moves

Most people sell what grows to pay for what they need, and lose the growth. The old families never did that. They held the asset, borrowed against it, and handed it down. This protocol puts that habit into a vault anyone can open.

I

Grow

Hold an asset that compounds and keep holding it. Staked ether (wstETH) earns its own yield inside the vault, year after year, whether you borrow or not. The vault never touches it except to protect itself.

What does not happen: nobody sells your collateral for you unless it falls below the safety line you can read on the screen.

II

Borrow

Draw OCT, the protocol's loan unit, against your wstETH. The interest is fixed at 1.5% a year for the life of the deployment. There is no application, no credit file and no bank. You choose how much to draw; the protocol only enforces the floor of 110% collateral.

OCT is what you borrow and what you repay. It is minted when you borrow and burned when you repay.

III

Pass it on

Name up to ten heirs and their shares. Every action you take with your key counts as a sign of life. After one year of silence and thirty more days of grace, anyone can trigger the settlement: the vault pays its own debt at par, then delivers what is left straight into your heirs' wallets.

No lawyer, no custodian, no death certificate. Just a clock, and a plan you can change at any time.

Plain words

  • The protocol holds no keys. Nobody can pause it, upgrade it, change the rate or take the collateral. Nobody can help you either: write your heirs down.
  • Borrowing has a 0.5% mint fee. Redemptions have a 0.75% floor fee. Fees and interest feed the Redemption Buffer, which protects borrowers from redemptions and sets how much the system may lend.
  • If your collateral ratio falls under 110%, your vault can be liquidated. Keep a margin; the dashboard shows your liquidation price.
  • This page is a test deployment of unaudited code on a test network. It teaches the mechanics. It is not an offer, not advice and not a promise.

Operator (optional)

An operator may add collateral and repay for you. If you allow it, it may also borrow and withdraw, always to your wallet, as long as the vault keeps the ratio floor you set. Operator actions never count as your sign of life.

Execute a plan (anyone)

When a wallet's plan is executable, anyone may call the settlement. The caller is paid a small reward from the estate. If the system cannot settle the whole debt at par today, the call does what it can and can be repeated.

Enter a wallet to read its state.

Redemption Buffer

A wstETH pot inside the protocol. Every fee and all interest end up here. Redemptions are served from the pot first, so vaults are touched only when it is empty. The pot also sets the debt ceiling: $1,000,000 plus $100 for every $1 it holds.

Redeem OCT

Exchange OCT for collateral at the oracle price, minus the redemption fee. Minimum 5,000 OCT. The buffer serves first.

Fill the pot

Anyone may add wstETH to the pot. It creates no claim; it strengthens every vault.

Stability Pool

OCT deposited here absorbs liquidated debt and receives the liquidated collateral in exchange, at a discount. Your deposit is part of your succession plan.
